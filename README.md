## About me

I'm a full-stack software engineer and AI solutions architect with **15+ years of experience**, currently building on **Microsoft Copilot Studio, ReactJS and Power Platform** at Microsoft.

I enjoy turning complex problems into useful software, whether that means designing an architecture, building an interface, connecting systems, or helping another engineer find a way forward.

Today, I'm part of Microsoft's **Copilot Acceleration Team (CAT)**, working on enterprise AI agents and the tools that help teams build, understand, and govern them. My foundation is in **C#/.NET, React, and Azure**, and I like staying close to the code while thinking about the bigger system.

Learning by building is a thread through my work: explore a concept, make it work, understand the trade-offs, and share what I learn.

## My journey

My engineering journey started in **2011**, building banking integrations and web applications. Since then, I've worked across higher education, health insurance, payroll, and now enterprise AI.

Before joining **Microsoft in 2025**, I spent more than six years at **ADP**, building next-generation payroll experiences with React, Node.js, and .NET. Earlier roles at **Accenture, CSDC, and Tatwa Technologies** shaped how I approach integration, product development, and leading engineering teams.
